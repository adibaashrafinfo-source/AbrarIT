## Keyword tiers

- **Primary** — the main keyword each page is built to rank for (used in title, H1, meta description)
- **Secondary** — supporting phrases used naturally in body copy and headings
- **Intent** — what the searcher wants (helps judge if a page is the right match)

## Why "Bangladesh" / "Dhaka" everywhere

Abrar IT is a local service business, not a global SaaS product — the overwhelming majority of realistic buyers are searching with a location qualifier ("website design **company in Bangladesh**", not just "website design"). Competing for the bare keyword against global agencies with years of domain authority is a losing fight; competing for the localized version is winnable and matches exactly who you actually serve. This is also precisely what the `areaServed` and `PostalAddress` schema markup reinforces for Google and AI systems.
