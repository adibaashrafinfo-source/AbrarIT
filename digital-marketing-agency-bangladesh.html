Running ads without a clear strategy is one of the most common ways Bangladeshi businesses lose money online. Boosting a post is not a marketing strategy — it's a shortcut that rarely produces a predictable return.

Abrar IT runs digital marketing campaigns built on a simple principle: every taka spent should be traceable to a result. That means proper audience and creative testing, clear tracking of cost-per-lead and return on ad spend, and campaigns structured around your actual sales funnel — not just vanity metrics like likes and reach.

We work across Facebook and Instagram advertising, Google Ads, and organic SEO, tailored to where your specific customers actually spend their time and how they actually search. For a local business, that might mean hyper-local Facebook targeting; for an e-commerce brand, that might mean a mix of paid social and search intent. We build the strategy around your business, not a one-size-fits-all package.
