Most businesses in Bangladesh treat email and SMS as an occasional announcement channel. Done properly, they're closer to a second sales team — working automatically, every day, without anyone manually sending a thing.

Abrar IT designs and automates the email and SMS flows that quietly compound revenue for e-commerce and service businesses: welcome sequences for new subscribers, abandoned cart recovery, post-purchase cross-sells, and win-back campaigns for customers who've gone quiet. Each flow is built once, triggered automatically by customer behavior, and refined over time based on real open and click data.

For businesses selling in Bangladesh, SMS remains an especially high-open-rate channel alongside email — we build both into one coordinated strategy rather than treating them as separate tools, so a customer gets the right message on the right channel at the right moment.
