Static images are easy to scroll past. Motion catches the eye first, which is exactly why short animated content now outperforms static posts across almost every social platform used in Bangladesh.

Abrar IT produces motion graphics for brands that want their logo, product or message to feel alive — animated logo reveals for intros and outros, explainer videos that simplify a product or service in under a minute, and short branded motion pieces built specifically for how people actually watch video on their phones: vertical, captioned, and fast-paced from the first second.

Every motion project starts with a clear script or storyboard before any animation begins, so the final video actually communicates the right message — not just moves nicely. We deliver in the formats you need for each platform, from square Instagram posts to vertical Reels and Shorts.
